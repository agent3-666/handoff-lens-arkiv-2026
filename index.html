BOUNDED EVIDENCE

Know what a green result means.

It proves that the designated wallets published matching statements in the visible records. It does not prove work quality, payment, legal identity, or a complete history.

Readonly entities cannot be edited, but their owners can still delete them. Entity Expiration is finite. Missing roots and unavailable RPCs produce “unverifiable.”
